Research Experience

### Graduate Research Assistant, Applied Algorithm Laboratory, School of Computing, University of Connecticut (August 2022 – present)

* Research on scalable record linkage, entity resolution, string algorithms, and high-performance computing for large-scale data integration.
* Developed a linear-time algorithm (O(l<sub>1</sub> + l<sub>2</sub>)) for the Jaro similarity metric, improving on its classical quadratic-time formulation.
* Developed bit-parallel and GPU-accelerated record-linkage algorithms that process millions of records and billions of candidate comparisons, achieving more than 100× end-to-end speedup over the CPU baseline.
* Developed GPU string-similarity algorithms achieving approximately 381×–702× speedup over CPU implementations in isolated similarity-computation experiments.
* Designed scalable blocking, pruning, and candidate-generation techniques that reduce the computational cost of large-scale record linkage while preserving matching accuracy.
* Developing cluster-quality metrics and optimization techniques for evaluating and improving record-linkage results, including identifying erroneous assignments in noisy and incomplete data.
* Designing parallel algorithms for GPU and multicore architectures, including workload scheduling, persistent GPU kernels, shared-memory optimization, and parallel similarity computation.
* Research on communication-efficient distributed quantum computing, including optimized communication strategies for the Quantum Fourier Transform and Grover's search.
