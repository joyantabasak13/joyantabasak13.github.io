---
title: "Record Linkage Quality Without Ground Truth"
excerpt: "Cluster-quality metrics and local-search optimization for evaluating and refining record-linkage results when true matches are unknown."
collection: projects
---
In practice, the true matches in a linked dataset are rarely known. This project develops cluster-quality metrics that evaluate record-linkage output without ground truth, and scalable local-search algorithms that use these metrics to find and correct erroneous record assignments in noisy and incomplete data.

* "Using Cluster Quality Metrics for Record Linkage Evaluation," ICCABS 2026 (forthcoming, Springer).
* Journal extension on unsupervised evaluation and refinement of record-linkage clusters, under review at the *Journal of Computational Biology*.
