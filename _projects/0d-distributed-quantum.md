Distributed quantum computing splits a quantum algorithm across multiple devices, where communication between them is costly. This work designs problem-specific communication strategies for the Quantum Fourier Transform and Grover's search ([IEEE QCNC 2025](https://doi.org/10.1109/QCNC64685.2025.00069)).
